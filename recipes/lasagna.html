Lasagna Recipe

To make the meat sauce, brown ground beef with onions and garlic, then add tomato paste and crushed tomatoes and let it simmer for 20 minutes. For the béchamel, melt butter, whisk in flour, then slowly add milk until you get a thick creamy sauce. Cook your lasagna sheets until just soft.

Layer in a baking dish: meat sauce, pasta sheets, béchamel, and repeat until everything is used up. Finish with a generous layer of mozzarella and parmesan on top, then bake at 180°C (350°F) for 45 minutes until golden and bubbling. Let it rest for 10 minutes before cutting so it holds its shape.

Ingredients:

  • Ground beef (500g)
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Tomato paste
  • Crushed tomatoes
  • Flour
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Pasta sheets
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Olive oil

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Make the meat sauce by browning ground beef with onions and garlic, then adding tomato paste and crushed tomatoes and letting it simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Make the béchamel by melting butter, whisking in flour, then slowly adding milk until you get a thick creamy sauce.
  4. Cook the lasagna sheets according to the package instructions.
  5. Layer the baking dish with meat sauce, pasta sheets, and béchamel.
  6. Top with a generous layer of mozzarella and parmesan cheese.
  7. Bake for 45 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  8. Let it rest for 10 minutes before cutting.
